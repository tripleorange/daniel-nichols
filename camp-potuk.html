FORTHCOMING / MIDDLE-GRADE FANTASY MYSTERY

The Secret of
Camp Potuk

Twelve-year-old Charlie Cole does not want to go to Camp Potuk. Then she learns Penny January will be in her cabin. Someone, Charlie decides, has to keep an eye on her.

When Mila’s grandmother’s compass disappears from a locked footlocker and other missing valuables turn up on Heron’s steps, a rival cabin blames the girls. Charlie sets out to find the thief with Penny, handbook-loving Mila, and Dez, who writes down everything he sees. His notebook holds one very odd detail: there are twelve chickens in the coop, but he has seen a thirteenth.

Wet footprints, mysterious drums, and a bear that leaves bird tracks lead them toward a secret the campfire stories have only partly right. The creature at the heart of it is frightened of something beneath the lake. Charlie promises to keep him safe. But he has his own idea of what those words mean, and he expects her to make good on them by bonfire night.

A middle-grade fantasy mystery set in the world of The Chronicles of Eridul.
