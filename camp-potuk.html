FORTHCOMING / MIDDLE-GRADE FANTASY MYSTERY

The Secret of
Camp Potuk

Charlie arrives at Camp Potuk hoping for a summer with her friend Penny, not another mystery. Then valuables vanish from locked cabins, a wet bear leaves impossible tracks, and the girls of Heron Cabin take the blame. With a careful young investigator named Dez, rule-minded Mila, and a chipmunk with a habit of repeating the wrong words, Charlie follows the clues to a ruined island chapel and a frightened creature who can never quite keep the same shape.

Helping him should be simple. But a promise made too quickly ties the children to a stolen trophy and a danger reaching across the lake. To clear their cabin and bring their new friend home, they must learn the difference between keeping someone safe and claiming someone as their own.

A middle-grade fantasy mystery about friendship, belonging, and the trouble with promises, set in the world of The Chronicles of Eridul.
