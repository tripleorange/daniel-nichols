Books open for beta readers

These are unpublished manuscripts shared in confidence for comment. Each opens with the password you were given, and the same password opens both, so if you finish one and would like to take on the other, it is here.

  • A Use for Good Men

    A novel · literary suspense · for adult readers

    In 2003, Sam Artell was a Navy chaplain with the Marines in Iraq, the man they found when they needed someone to hear what they could tell no one else. Nineteen years later, a defense contractor wants the software he built to help veterans find work, and the executive behind the deal was waiting at the end of the worst road of Sam’s war.

    About 36 chapters. Themes of war, moral injury and family.

  • The Secret of Camp Potuk

    A middle-grade mystery · ages 8 to 12 · The Chronicles of Eridul

    Twelve-year-old Charlie Cole does not want to spend two weeks at Camp Potuk. Then she finds out that Penny January is going as well. A compass disappears from a locked footlocker, the rival cabin says Heron has sticky fingers, and the trail leads to a creature the campfire stories have only partly right. Charlie promises to keep him safe. He takes the promise to mean something she never intended.

    27 short chapters, about 48,000 words. Fine to read aloud with a kid; their reactions are welcome comments too.

How commenting works

Inside a book, select any passage and choose Comment, or use the link at the end of a chapter. Your comments go to the author as you save them and are also kept on your device, so you can change them later. Tick “Remember me” on the lock screen and the book opens without the password next time.

Anything is useful: where you got confused, where you got bored, where you laughed, where you stopped believing it. You do not need to fix the sentence; pointing at it is enough.

Please do not copy the text or pass the link on. Questions: daniel@danielnichols.net.
